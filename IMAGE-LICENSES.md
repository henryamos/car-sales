# Image Licenses & Compliance Record — AutoGhana

This document records the provenance, licensing, commercial-use permissions, and source metadata for all photographic assets utilized across the **AutoGhana** platform ([autoghana.vercel.app](https://autoghana.vercel.app)).

---

## 1. Commercial Use Policy & Standards

1. **Commercial Use Authorization**: All decorative and editorial photographs must carry explicit commercial-use grants (e.g. Unsplash License, Pexels License, Pixabay Content License, or Creative Commons with commercial permissions).
2. **No Unlicensed Media**: Random Google Images search downloads, Pinterest reposts, and unverified social media scrapes are strictly forbidden.
3. **No Third-Party Dealership Branding**: Photographs must not feature competing dealership logos or signage (such as Toyota Ghana) that could mislead consumers or imply unauthorized business affiliations.
4. **Vehicle Inventory Integrity**:
   - Every listing must feature accurate photography of the specific model and generation (e.g., distinguishing the 2024 Prado 250 from the 2021 Prado J150).
   - For real production listings, only authentic photographs provided directly by the verified seller, importer, or dealership with written consent may be displayed.
5. **Audit Trail**: Every asset source, license type, photographer credit, and download date must be tracked in this registry.
